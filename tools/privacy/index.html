Privacy policy for Longcut's analytics tool

Last updated 5 October 2026.

What this is

Longcut runs a small private tool that reads analytics for its own YouTube channel, @longcut_io. It is used only by the channel's owner, to learn which parts of a video work and to make the next one better. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • YouTube Analytics reports for the Longcut channel's own videos, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and device types.
  • Basic details of the channel's own videos, such as title, publish date and length.

Access is granted by the channel owner through Google sign-in and is read-only: the tool cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

What it stores

The aggregate numbers above are saved on the channel owner's own computer. YouTube Analytics reports are aggregated across viewers; the tool does not collect, receive or store information about individual viewers. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time in your Google account's security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Longcut YouTube channel.
